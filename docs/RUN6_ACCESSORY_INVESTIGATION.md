## Correction decision

Two diagnostic alternatives were prototyped without changing production code:

1. A wider exterior edge band recovers more synthetic accessory outline but
   remains too sparse on Run 6.
2. Low-contrast dark-region recovery with morphological closing recovers the
   synthetic body and connector, but on Run 6 it also captures the hat, dark
   garments, footwear, and duplicate silhouette boundaries as one merged
   region.

The second alternative violates the existing exterior-band suppression
contract and cannot be made source-general with the available deterministic
evidence. It was rejected rather than shipped.

## Decision and next boundary

The first loss is **foreground classification plus rendered-detail candidate
generation for dark accessory regions against a similar-colored background**.
The remaining failure is not a Cut Line ownership, support-canvas scale, PDF,
SVG, or Color Guide lifecycle problem.

This ticket therefore stops without a production correction. Run 6 remains a
truthful digital Fail. A future attempt needs additional semantic information
or an explicit maker-guided accessory selection/annotation; another global
contrast or component heuristic would be unsafe under the current product
boundary.
